1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & HEADLINE METRICS
================================================================================
Apple Inc. reported record September-quarter revenue and earnings per share for its fiscal 2026 fourth quarter ended September 26, 2026.

- Quarterly Revenue: $114.48 billion (reported as $114.5B), up 11.7% YoY (vs. $102.50 billion in Q4 FY2025)
- Full-Year FY2026 Revenue: $478.83 billion, up 15.0% YoY
- Quarterly Net Income: $30.70 billion, up 11.8% YoY
- Full-Year FY2026 Net Income: $132.20 billion, up 18.0% YoY
- Quarterly Diluted EPS: $2.10, up 13.5% YoY (vs. $1.85 in Q4 FY2025; beat consensus estimate of $1.98 by 6.06%)
- Full-Year FY2026 Diluted EPS: $8.98, up 20.0% YoY
- Quarterly Gross Margin: 47.9% (includes ~1.0 percentage point favorable impact from tariff refunds; underlying margin ~46.9% vs. 47.2% in Q4 FY2025)
- Quarterly Operating Margin: 31.1%
- Quarterly Dividend Declared: $0.27 per share (0.31% annualized yield; ~12% payout ratio)

4. KEY OPERATING, CASH FLOW & BALANCE SHEET HIGHLIGHTS
================================================================================
- Research & Development (R&D): Rose 32.0% YoY in Q4 to $11.70 billion; full-year FY2026 R&D reached a record $45.77 billion (+32.0% YoY) driven by AI initiatives.
- Capital Expenditures (CapEx): $9.00 billion for full-year FY2026, reflecting reliance on third-party partners for external compute capacity.
- Free Cash Flow (FCF): $136.10 billion for full-year FY2026, up 38.0% YoY (Q4 operating cash flow declined 5.5% YoY).
- Share Repurchases & Share Count: Diluted shares outstanding declined 1.6% during FY2026 from 14,773 million to 14,532 million, driving EPS growth (+20%) ahead of net income growth (+18%).
- Stock-Based Compensation: $13.80 billion for FY2026 (~2.9% of total revenue).

================================================================================
5. MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY & LEADERSHIP CONTEXT
================================================================================
- Leadership Transition: Q4 FY2026 marked the first earnings report led by CEO John Ternus, who succeeded Tim Cook on September 1, 2026.
- Product Cycle & Pricing: Q4 results included only eight days of initial iPhone 18 Pro sales and preceded retail availability of the new foldable iPhone Duo. Results reflected a full quarter of across-the-board price increases implemented in June 2026 (including a $100 increase on iPhones) to offset component and memory supply inflation.
- Supply Constraints: CEO John Ternus noted ongoing supply constraints and display/component bottlenecks affecting the iPhone Duo rollout.

================================================================================
6. Q1 FY2027 FORWARD GUIDANCE & RISK FACTORS
================================================================================
- Q1 FY2027 (December Quarter) Revenue Growth Guidance: +7.0% to +10.0% YoY (above consensus estimate of +6.1%).
- Q1 FY2027 Services Revenue Growth Guidance: +8.0% to +9.0% YoY.
- Q1 FY2027 Gross Margin Guidance: 45.0% to 46.0% (below consensus estimate of 46.3%, reflecting ongoing memory price inflation and ramping AI infrastructure/R&D costs).
- Key Risk Factors Cited: Component and memory cost inflation, supply chain constraints on newly launched hardware, and reliance on third-party providers for cloud/AI compute infrastructure.
