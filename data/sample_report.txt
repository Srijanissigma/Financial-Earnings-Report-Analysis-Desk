Northwind Technologies Q3 FY2026 Earnings Release
Revenue for the quarter was $4.82 billion, up 12.4% year over year.
Net income was $612 million, compared with $540 million a year ago.
Gross margin was 41.5%, down from 43.2% a year ago.
Operating margin was 18.1%.
Diluted EPS was $2.31.
Cloud segment revenue grew 22% to $1.9 billion.
Inventory increased 19% from the prior quarter.
Free cash flow decreased 8% year over year.
The top three customers represented 34% of revenue.
Foreign currency headwinds of $85 million affected results.
Supply chain constraints in the hardware business limited shipments.
The company disclosed ongoing litigation related to a patent dispute, with damages not yet estimable.
For the fourth quarter, the company expects revenue of $5.0 billion to $5.2 billion and full-year revenue growth of 11% to 13%.
Management raised full-year guidance for operating margin to 18% to 19%.
